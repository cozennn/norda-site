Pacely

A clinic app with a drag-and-drop schedule and recovery charts

A clinic management app for a physiotherapy or rehab practice, shown with sample data from a five-clinician clinic. Good for: physiotherapy, sports-rehab, chiropractic or any appointment-based clinic that tracks patients, visits, treatment plans and billing in one place. How it works: drag a visit on the clinic floor to another time or another clinician and every booking moves into place, open a patient to see their pain and movement scores as a chart, and drag a patient on the plans board when they are ready for the next phase. Includes: a today page with headline figures, the day's schedule by clinician, who is up next with check-in, patients worth a second look, caseload by body area and a waitlist, a full schedule with open gaps and a weekly capacity grid, patients in care with filters and a chart panel with notes, plan, home exercises and visits, a waitlist that books a first visit in one click, discharged patients, a treatment plan board and an exercise library you assign from, outcomes over twelve months with a CSV download, clinician cards and an editable rota, invoices with a PAID stamp, insurance claims with a status track and resubmit, a patient inbox, settings, a support page, search with ⌘K, forms to book visits, add patients and draft invoices, a sidebar that folds into a slim icon bar, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, and a patient's reply in the inbox is simulated, so connect your own data and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
