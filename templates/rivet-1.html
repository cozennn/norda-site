Rivet

A nine-page personal finance app, black and neon or light

A complete personal finance app with nine working pages and a black and neon look, plus a light theme. Good for: banking apps, budgeting apps, money trackers and fintech products. How it works: every number comes from one set of sample data, so adding a payment, freezing a card or funding a goal updates every page, and changes are saved in the browser. Includes: an overview, wallets with cards you can freeze and cap, a searchable ledger with bank sync and monthly statements, cash flow with a 30-day forecast and unused subscriptions, budgets and savings goals, an investment portfolio with buy and sell, a lessons library, a help desk with tickets, settings, search, notifications, and layouts for desktop, tablet and phone. How to use: comes with sample data, so swap the store file for your own data or backend. Built with TypeScript and Vite, and uses GSAP for animation.
