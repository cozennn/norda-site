Tilvo

A business money app with a drag-and-drop cash planner

A money app for a small business that sees its cash, plans its payments and chases what it is owed, shown with sample data from an independent record label and studio. Good for: a small company, studio, agency or label that wants its bank accounts, planned payments, cards, invoices and reports in one place. How it works: drag a planned payment to another day and the balance forecast redraws as you drag, a red band shows where cash dips under your safety buffer, the lowest point moves to the new low, and a side panel suggests the move that fixes it. Includes: an overview with cash across accounts, money in and out, the next 30 days and recent activity, a cash planner over two weeks, six weeks or a quarter with a list view, a royalty run with an approval track and advances to recoup, payees, accounts and savings pots with money moves, an activity list you can re-categorise and attach receipts to, monthly statements as CSV, cards you can turn over, freeze and set limits on, card spend and missing receipts, income by source, invoices with send, remind and a PAID stamp, subscriptions and services with pause and cancel, a report builder that switches measure and split with a CSV export, budgets you can adjust, settings, a help page, search with ⌘K, a top bar that folds to icons, light and dark mode, phone, tablet and desktop. How to use: it runs on sample data saved in the browser with no backend, so connect your own bank data and server. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
