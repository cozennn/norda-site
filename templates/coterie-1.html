A people and payroll dashboard for HR teams

A three-screen dashboard for managing staff, work hours and pay. Good for: HR tools, team apps and internal company dashboards. How it works: switch between Overview, People and Payroll, track hours with a working timer, tick off onboarding tasks, search the team and invite new people. Includes: an hours chart, a week calendar, a benefits list, payroll per person and downloadable PDF payslips, in a light theme for desktop, tablet and phone. How to use: replace the sample data in app.js with your own data or backend. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and uses GSAP for animation.
