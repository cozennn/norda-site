Finance dashboards

Every dashboard about money: Fathom, a live trading terminal with eight pages; Rivet, a nine-page personal finance app in black and neon with a light theme, cards you freeze and cap, a ledger with bank sync, a 30-day forecast, budgets, goals and a portfolio with buy and sell; Meridian, a portfolio that moves like a single instrument; Coffer, eight pages of invoicing and accounting where recording a payment stamps the invoice PAID; Rivolo, an eleven-page money app for freelancers with every pot on one bar, safe-to-spend shown as its sum, a two-sided money-flow chart and a tax set-aside on every client payment; Kesta, a ten-page production-finance app for a film studio with a cash-bridge waterfall, four currency wallets and budgets per production; Tillo, a ten-page money app for a bike shop with a cash-flow chart you drag across to total any run of months, a three-step send flow and cards that frost and turn over; Kofra, an eleven-page business bank account for a ceramics studio with a balance runway that forecasts 30 days, invoices that stamp PAID and feed a tax jar, and a hold-to-send payment flow; Sovra, an eleven-page business bank account for a florist with a cash-flow chart you drag across to total any stretch, team cards that frost when frozen and a VAT return worked out against its pot; Kinby, a ten-page family money app with a swipe-carousel wallet of everyone's cards, kids' cards on weekly limits, a bills calendar with paydays and goals that fill as jars; Obolo, a thirteen-page business-finance app for an olive-oil exporter with four currency wallets, a money in and out chart, a live converter and invoices that stamp PAID; Grista, an eleven-page business bank account for a three-shop bakery with its cash drawn day by day, the big days pinned and a year you drag through, team cards that lock with a padlock and a what-if cash forecast; Orvel, a twelve-page finance console for a vinyl pressing plant with every payment drawn as a dot you brush to filter the ledger, a review queue dealt as a stack, invoices that stamp PAID and bills that take two sign-offs; Corvane, an eleven-section payments operations portal for a card processor with a threshold lab you drag across last month's risk scores, a review queue for held payments, a disputes board, payouts and an audit log of every action; Rivlet, a twenty-five-page finance and HR back office for three climbing gyms with a sort desk you drag card charges into their categories on, cash across four accounts, cards that freeze, a profit and loss statement and tax filings that pay from the reserve; Kinlo, an eleven-section finance and HR back office for a two-site cinema with takings by tickets, bar and hire that morph between ranges, card payments filed in bulk, invoices that stamp PAID, a VAT return and a pay run you approve and pay; Coffra, a twenty-four-page finance and people back office for three climbing gyms with a cash-flow chart that morphs between ranges and gyms, a month-end close that ticks itself, transactions filed by suggestion, cards that frost when frozen and a pay run you approve; Ledra, an eight-section money, people and paperwork back office for a landscape architecture studio with a cash chart whose three-month forecast four what-if switches reshape, card charges you drag into their categories, invoices that stamp PAID, bills you approve and pay and a four-step pay run; Covry, an eleven-section money and people back office for a group of veterinary clinics in frosted glass, with a cash chart that morphs between months, weeks and one clinic, card payments you file from a stack with always-file rules, invoices that stamp PAID, a pay run you approve and contracts signed on a drawn pad; Talvo, a thirteen-page business-finance app for a craft brewery with a 90-day cash forecast you drag bills and invoices across, bills that stamp PAID and a treasury ladder; and Tilvo, a nine-page money app for an independent record label with a cash planner you drag payments across while the balance re-draws, cards that frost when frozen, a royalty run you approve, invoices that stamp PAID and a report builder. Calm, transform-only motion, ready for your own numbers.
