Love & celebrations

Pages made to be sent to someone: a pixel-art love letter, a birthday film, candles to blow out, a long-distance date, a heart built from a thousand "i love you"s. Personal, playful and easy to make your own.

  • Love Letter — A pixel art love letter opened with an arrow
  • Love — A Will you be mine page where No runs away
  • Birthday — An animated birthday greeting that ends in a heart of flowers
  • Date — A cute video call where two friends blow kisses
  • Posy — A birthday card where you hand over a flower
  • Wish — A birthday cake whose candles you blow out with your breath
  • Heartcode — A 3D heart made of the words i love you
  • Pulse — A glowing heart made of connected dots
  • Aurelia — Three flowers that grow and bloom at night
